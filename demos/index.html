World Models and Robot Grandmas: demos

Small, readable versions of the 3D demos from the blog post Intro to World Models and Robot Grandmas. Each one is a single page: open it, then read its source.

  • BlobOne Gaussian splat: a soft, see-through blob of colour. No download.
  • CatA Gaussian Splatting scan, drawn from scratch: decoding, sorting and blending the blobs. 11 MB.
  • Apartment worldA world generated with World Labs Marble; fly through it. 8 MB.
  • MeshesMarble's collider mesh next to its detailed mesh. 3 MB.
  • Robot GrandmaThe game from the post: a robot walking with a cane, physics in MuJoCo, room in splats. 13 MB (11 MB on phones). Source code coming soon.
Code: MIT. The cat scan: "Kitty" by Andrej Kurcik, CC BY 4.0. The apartment and its meshes: generated with World Labs Marble from photos by Max Vakhtbovych (Pexels), cleaned up by Sean Tey; CC0, no rights reserved. Details in the repository's README and THIRD_PARTY.md.
